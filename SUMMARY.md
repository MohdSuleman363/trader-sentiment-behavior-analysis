# Project Summary  Trader Behavior vs Market Sentiment

## Methodology

Trading history was merged with daily Fear & Greed sentiment data.

Performance evaluated using:

* Profitability (PnL)
* Win rate
* Drawdown proxy

Behavioral patterns analyzed:

* Trading frequency
* Position sizing
* Long vs Short bias

Traders were segmented into behavioral groups, followed by a predictive model estimating trade profitability and clustering to identify trader archetypes.

---

## Key Insights

Greed markets produce the most stable profits due to trending conditions.

Fear markets increase trading activity but reduce efficiency, indicating emotional trading behavior.

Model results show trade execution decisions influence profitability more than sentiment alone.

Most traders are active but fail to generate consistent profits.

---

## Strategy Recommendations

Reduce trading frequency during Fear periods.

Allow aggressive strategies only in trending markets.

Reduce position size during Extreme Greed to avoid late entries.

Focus on trade quality rather than quantity.

---

## Conclusion

Market sentiment influences trader behavior, but disciplined execution determines long-term profitability.
